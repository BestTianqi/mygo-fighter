# Viola (id 15)

The first normal-proportion 2D fighting assets for Viola from _BanG Dream! YUME∞MITA_. Drawn with the built-in **imagegen**; the transparent originals stay in this directory, and packing only cuts out complete connected figures, scales them to a uniform world height and sets a foot anchor, never stitching limbs or stretching a pose.

## Official design references

- [MewType official “Character CM: Viola”](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV11uK36xEs2/), 31 seconds, cid `39994722827`.
- The 4-second profile card shows the complete outfit, and 8 s and 20 s show the face; the source video and the verification contact sheet are cached in `output/viola-art/`.
- Long dark forest-green hair, a single bun and a hime cut on the left, black crossed clips on the right and silver-white triangular and bar clips on the left; olive-gold eyes and a small mole below the right of the mouth. Charcoal-brown school uniform, a cream rectangular chest panel with a flower-shaped badge, a gray-purple finely pleated stand collar, gold piping with four waist buttons, a short pleated skirt, black ankle socks and olive loafers.
- Uses the anime school uniform look; `virtual` in the code is an existing character asset grouping key and does not mean a new virtual performance outfit was added.

The three J stages, the heavy attack and U/I/O/P all use distinct key art. Transition frames such as startup, recovery, low and airborne reuse these complete drawings through the explicit aliases in `manifest.json`; the aliases are not counted as new drawings. Every body part has genuine transparent margins, both shoes are complete, and actions switch by whole-atlas frames.

Rebuild: `python tools/build_viola_assets.py`. The full game build calls `install_viola_assets(assets)` before output and installs only `characters[15]`, `mewtypeForms['15']` and `mewtypePortraits['15']`, without overwriting older characters; the super artwork is installed by the existing finish asset builder.

## Prompt set

Below are the production constraints and final prompt points used by the built-in imagegen this time, so later work can stay consistent:

1. **Full-body portrait**: `Production full-body portrait and idle sprite of Viola / ビオラ from BanG Dream! YUME∞MITA, matching the official profile and close-up references. Normal seven-head-tall anatomy, slim human proportions, not chibi. Preserve dark forest-green waist-length hair, single left bun, hime-cut bangs, black crossed right clips and silver triangular/bar left clips, olive-gold eyes and tiny right beauty mark. Exact charcoal/ivory/lavender/gold school outfit, pleated skirt, black ankle socks, olive loafers. Composed sly smile, small dark smartphone, complete head/hair/hands/shoes. Clean cel-shaded anime line art, consistent subdued palette. Centered isolated whole figure, genuine transparent background, no text, instruments, floor, shadow or glow.`
2. **Locomotion and guard sheet**: `Eight independent complete full-body poses of the same reference Viola, 4 columns × 2 rows, transparent gutters, facing screen right. Top: walking contact, walking passing, forward dash, standing guard. Bottom: crouching guard, rising jump, recoiling hit reaction, side-lying knockout. Normal anatomy, consistent face/outfit and palette, no cropped body, no labels or shared effects.`
3. **Attack sheet**: `Eight different complete attack poses, same exact character, outfit and normal proportions, 4 × 2 transparent grid. Top: short open-palm first jab, twisting horizontal backhand second jab, rising elbow third finisher, forward side kick heavy. Bottom: eye-level smartphone recording U, crouching smartphone edit I, long lunging dismissal palm O, raised-smartphone commanding P. All target screen right, fully visible hair/hands/shoes, no extra instruments, glow, text or background.`
4. **Direction fix**: `Precise sprite sheet correction: redraw only row1/column1 and row2/column1 toward screen right, with back/hair at left and forward palm or phone at right. Preserve six other figures, cell layout, character identity, colors, full bodies and real alpha background. Keep the rejected v1 as archive; install corrected v2.`

Skill props and casting poses are fighting-game adaptations and are not a setting where the character has superpowers in the anime.

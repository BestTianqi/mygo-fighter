# Combat Tempo and Voice Adjustments

- Anon TGW: sourced from [爱音のTGW丰川集团](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1srFZecETK/), uploaded by「编新述旧」, part 1, cid 28169736175, trimmed from 27.15–30.85 s. The original's character, subtitles and audio were checked, and the clip is wired in as the independent `sonic` (↓＋I) voice `meme_0_tgw`. The original source, timings and hashes are in sources.json.
- The Tomori penguin P restores the old audio from `recordings/penguin-p.mp3`; it plays at original speed, and the old video is kept and played at original speed as well. Five layers are emitted at 50/230/410/590/770 ms, each dealing a fixed 10 damage when not guarded; the attack's recovery totals 1500 ms, so the audio can finish naturally.
- Uika's normal form and stage form P lasts 1000 ms in total; it starts after 80 ms and crawls in the direction of the input at 600 world units per second; 10 damage each at 100/280/460/640/820 ms, with no combo scaling. The crawl is blocked by bodies, terrain and platform edges. Holding crouch guard keeps low guard.
- Uika's finish art is a 3.6-second excerpt (at 53.8 s in the original) from the existing [super source video](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1oLVXzFEUW/), cropped to x=300/y=150/w=240/h=160, and is used for both outfits' round-win screens.
- Every super other than Anon's has its action timing halved, and Uika's battle duration is 1 second. All super videos and audio restore the original files and play at original speed, and they do not speed up with the skill's cast. Video and super voice are independent of recovery and of later normal attacks: they play out and end naturally, resume after a pause, and are cleared on character swap, restart and round end. Soyo's control still lasts 2 seconds, and round settlement keeps its original duration.
- All normal-attack damage is multiplied by 0.75 and rounded to an integer (heavy-attack characters' values are adjusted in step), and recovery is lengthened by 80 ms. The attacker now gains +2 resonance on a landed skill hit, normal-attack hits were reverted to +12, guard gives +1, and supers and summons still give no energy; the defender's energy gain keeps its original setting. Every dash/blink costs 10 resonance, deducted only when it actually activates.

The audio cache is generated by tools/build_meme_assets.py, and stage metadata is recorded in manifest.json. The old source material is kept for provenance.

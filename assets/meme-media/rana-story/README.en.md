# Rana: Anime Voices and the Finale Clip

Seven real anime voice clips were taken from the official 30-second 「角色介绍CM・要楽奈篇」 (character introduction CM, Rana edition). The original publisher is [BanG Dream Channel☆](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YE6sJdXyrKo), and the [official character page](https://anime.bang-dream.com/mygo/character/rana/) confirms the voice actor as Hina Aoki. This download uses [花园多惠Official's reupload of the original CM, part 3](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1i14y1U7DP/?p=3), CID `1173143631`; the reuploading account is not an official account, and the record clearly separates the original publisher from the download source.

↓＋U's launcher reuses U; the airborne ↓＋O dive reuses バイバイ; ↓＋I's tuning reuses 満足. All clips keep their original speed and complete short sentences, with no AI voice or streamer narration. The speaker was verified by the white short hair, the gold-and-blue heterochromatic eyes, the original shot and the visible lip sync, and a Japanese word-level transcript helped place the cuts. One short line in the CM whose automatic transcription was unclear was not used, and no original dialogue was invented from it.

P「结束了吗？·野猫终奏」's video and recording are both the continuous 23.15–26.60 s of the original CM: it cuts from a close-up of Rana's mouth to her full face holding the parfait, keeping both sentences and the pause between them. The video does not speed up with the battle's release, and the recording is not cut off by a follow-up skill. The finish art is Rana at 26.00 s of the same segment, stored at `assets/finish-art/rana-anime-ending.webp`.

`sources.json` records, segment by segment, the move, voice, BV/CID/part, timings, source, speaker evidence and SHA-256; `install.json` provides the slots/variants wiring reference for character 3. The old matcha parfait, interesting-woman and other material is kept, and the original character art and actions are not modified here. The parfait projectile, sound waves, slide kick and other effects are game adaptations, not new original dialogue or a claim of superpowers.

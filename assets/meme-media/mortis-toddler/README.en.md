# Mutsumi's Two-Years-Old / Burger Transformation-Only Skills

The audio comes from [我只有两岁！我要吃汉堡！](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV17uug6JEza/), by 埴安神袿姫, cid 40714699317, part 1. The fan-made Mutsumi footage and the subtitles in the original video were checked, and a Chinese transcript was used to verify the segment boundaries.

- Stage form ↓U「我只有两岁」: 20 resonance, 150 ms startup, a crying shock wave over 260 world units around her, 14 damage, 230 knockback, 1600 ms cooldown. The audio is trimmed to 0–1.5 s.
- Stage form ↓I「我要吃汉堡」: 25 resonance, 180 ms startup, fires a real burger projectile forward, speed 580, lifetime 1250 ms, 18 damage, 1400 ms cooldown. The audio is trimmed to 1.5–4.3 s and includes the original line and the crying that follows.

The existing stand-in, counter, string-pull and super are kept; the school-uniform form does not get these two derived moves. The two audio clips play independently and are packed into the offline HTML. A skill hit gives +2 resonance, a normal-attack hit restores +12, guarding still gives +1, and supers and summons give no energy. Source, timings and hashes are in sources.json.

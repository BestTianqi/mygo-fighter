# Taki: Anime Dialogue Replacement

Six anime dialogue clips were chosen from the official 30-second 「角色介绍CM・椎名立希篇」 (character introduction CM, Taki edition), voiced by Taki's own Koko Hayashi. The official original is [BanG Dream Channel☆](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UH6zMjECvQo), and the official [character page](https://anime.bang-dream.com/mygo/character/taki/) lets you check the CV and appearance; this download uses [花园多惠Official's reupload of the original CM, part 5](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1i14y1U7DP/?p=5), CID `1173143579`. That reuploading account is not an official account, and the source record clearly separates the original publisher from the reuploader.

Every frame was checked for Taki's dark brown long hair, purple eyes and lip sync, and Japanese word timestamps were used to re-check the cuts. The colleague's lines in the CM and Anon's ordering voice were excluded. No AI voice, streamer voice or MAD splicing was used. The sound waves, drum beats and character lines are this game's move adaptation; the original recordings do not claim these are superpowers in the anime.

P's recording and video are both the complete 2.83-second 19.55–22.38 s range of the original; the official CM already cuts there from a school-uniform close-up to the shop-staff scene, and this project keeps that original edit. Both sentences and the pause between them are kept, and audio and video stay at original speed, independent of the faster battle release. The finish art is a close-up of school-uniform Taki at 20.60 s of the same segment, see `assets/finish-art/taki-anime-composer.webp`.

Each segment's `sources.json` records `character:'taki'`, the explicit move, the voice source key, BV/CID/part, the original upload URL, the download reupload metadata, the speaker verification and SHA-256; `install.json` is separate wiring reference material and does not need to be loaded in the browser. The old 「哈？」 material is kept.

Directional/enhanced move suggestions: ↓＋U and its enhancement reuse U, 前＋I and its enhancement reuse O; the roll, charge, low sound wave and the eight-beat four-beat enhancement each reuse their own move's audio, and the extra low sound wave continues to use I. P must keep the complete recording playing and must not be cut off by the character's recovery action or a follow-up skill.

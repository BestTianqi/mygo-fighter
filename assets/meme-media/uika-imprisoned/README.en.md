# Uika's Transformation Music

Source: [Ave Mujica - Imprisoned XII (Official Music Video)](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Ep91YPEQo/), official account MyGO_AveMujica, cid 28735767349, part 1.

The first 0–20 s of the official MV are used as the H transformation music, keeping the original speed and pitch, with the background level adjusted and fades at both ends, bundled into the offline HTML. The source and the file hash are recorded in sources.json.

It plays once when Uika's H transformation succeeds, independent of the skill voices, and is not interrupted by normal attacks, skills or ordinary hits. Pausing or returning to the menu pauses the music, and it continues from where it stopped on resume; it stops when the transformation ends, on mute, on restart and at round end. Each of the two Uikas has its own channel. Other characters keep their original transformation sound.

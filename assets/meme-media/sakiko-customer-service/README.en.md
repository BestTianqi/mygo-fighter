# 客服小祥 · Please Wait (customer-service Sakiko)

Normal-form Sakiko ↓＋U (2P: ↓＋numpad 4), 20 resonance, 2.4 s cooldown. Fires one forward telephone sound wave at 180 ms; base damage 14, 550 ms hitstun, blockable, and restores 2 resonance on a normal hit. The existing normal-form U and the stage-form moves are kept.

The voice is taken from the customer-service Sakiko event of the official 2025 April Fools' 「ミネラル天然ライフ」. References: the [official event](https://bang-dream.com/mineralnaturallife/) and [Naohito_futa's subtitled screen recording](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1sGZbY6E8H/). Video part 1, cid 25779115564; 69.25–70.95 s, 「今しばらくお待ちください」 (please wait a moment). The cut was made after checking the subtitles, the screen recording and a Japanese transcript; original speed and pitch, with loudness normalisation and a very short fade in and out. The detailed source and the SHA256 are in sources.json.

customer-actions-source-v1.png was newly drawn by the built-in image_gen from Sakiko's official everyday-outfit reference. The three real actions are, in order, touching the headset, extending a palm to wait, and a polite bow; at build time the transparent character is separated and scaled for packing, and the existing action atlas is not replaced.

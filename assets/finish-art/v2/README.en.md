# Ultimate finish scene art v2

Generated by editing the original character art with the built-in image_gen, keeping the face shape, hairstyle and hair colour, eyes, outfit and body proportions, and extending it into a widescreen 2D scene with environmental depth of field and unified lighting. These are not character action textures and do not replace the original ultimate art used for ordinary knockouts.

- 21 separate widescreen images cover 16 characters and 22 battle forms; Tomori's normal and penguin forms share the 凑企鹅 image.
- Anon keeps the yellow milk dragon; Tomori keeps the penguin. Each of the five Mujica members has two images, one in normal outfit and one in stage outfit.
- The character sits on the left and the right side is left free for text; when the right-side player wins, the game mirrors the whole scene so the text always reads the right way round.
- The generated originals are PNG; at runtime they are exported as WebP at a maximum of 1600×900, and only the currently selected team is preloaded.
- The original character art, the old ultimate art, the battle action textures, the super videos and the super voices are all kept. Ordinary knockouts still use the original result image.

The complete prompts and reference image paths for each group are in `mygo-prompts.json`, `mujica-prompts.json`, `nyamu-prompts.json` and `mewtype-prompts.json`. Every image was made in the built-in image_gen edit mode and checked one by one.

There is one new finish voice per character, taken from the character's own lines in the original anime online; the sources, cut times, speakers and verification evidence are in [sources-voices.json](sources-voices.json), with notes in [voices/README.md](voices/README.md). Mujica's two forms and Tomori's penguin form share their own character's finish line. The voices keep the original speed, unlike the existing skill clips; if the full super line is still playing, the finish line queues up after it, so they never overlap or cut each other off.

Run `python tools/build_finish_assets.py` to update the runtime assets. `ultimateFinishes[character][form]` configures the scene image, the finish voice key, the Chinese line, the silent background video and the playback duration; `finishVoices` holds the finish audio tracks. The [background video notes](videos/README.md) and the [source clip records](sources-videos.json) refer to the same line. The ultimate art stays until both the original super voice and the finish voice have played out; in online play both sides finish before the next battle starts, and the damage rules are unchanged.
